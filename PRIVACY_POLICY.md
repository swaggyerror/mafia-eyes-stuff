# MAFIA EYES Privacy Policy

**Effective date: October 12, 2026**

MAFIA EYES is a Discord bot that monitors selected YouTube channels and posts update alerts in Discord servers that configure it. This policy explains what information the bot processes and how it is used.

## Information processed

**Discord server settings:** When a server configures the bot, MAFIA EYES stores that server's ID, the selected alert-channel ID, an optional role ID, and the server's alert preferences. These settings are used to send alerts to the configured channel and to apply the server's ping choices.

**Command interactions:** Discord provides the information needed to handle commands and settings interactions. This can include the invoking user's ID and server information. The bot uses this information to respond to commands and enforce access checks. It does not intentionally save command users' IDs as user profiles or collect the content of ordinary Discord messages. The bot does not enable Discord's Message Content, Presence, or Server Members privileged intents.

**YouTube information:** To detect updates, MAFIA EYES processes and stores YouTube channel and video information, including channel and video IDs, titles, playlist entries, availability, video counts, thumbnail data or fingerprints, and check times. It may also retrieve publicly available YouTube channel-page information to display public video counts.

## How information is used

Information is used only to operate the bot: remember server-specific settings, check tracked YouTube channels and videos, detect changes, deliver configured alerts, and diagnose service problems. MAFIA EYES does not sell Discord or YouTube data.

## Services and sharing

MAFIA EYES uses Discord to receive command interactions and send alerts, YouTube services and publicly available YouTube pages to retrieve tracking information, and Railway to host the bot and its persistent data. Information is sent to these services only as needed to operate the bot. Alerts may include YouTube links, video titles, thumbnails, and a mention of the role selected by the server.

## Storage and retention

The bot's state, including server settings and YouTube tracking data, is stored in its persistent data file on the bot host so it can survive restarts. Server settings are retained while needed to operate the service. **Removing the bot from a server does not currently automatically delete that server's saved settings.** A server administrator may request deletion using the contact method below; the operator will remove the server-specific settings from the active bot data.

## Requests and contact

For questions or to request deletion of a server's saved settings, contact the MAFIA EYES operator through the [official support server](https://discord.gg/NySpGetAZP). Identify the server whose settings are involved. Do not send passwords, bot tokens, or other authentication credentials.

## Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect the bot's data and credentials. No online service or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the bot changes. The effective date above will be updated when a revised version is published.
